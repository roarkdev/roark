The Product

A full patient-documentation workspace: paste in an after-visit summary, discharge note, or lab result, and work through it across six purpose-built tabs — translate it, review and edit the output, jot your own notes, and export or save your work.

Translate

Paste text directly, open a .txt file, or try a built-in example document — then generate a plain-language translation in one step.

Summary & Questions

Editable plain-language key points and a personalized appointment checklist — add, edit, or remove any point, and copy either list with one click.

Record

A dedicated space for the patient's own notes during the appointment — kept alongside the document it relates to.

Export

Download as PDF, print directly, save as plain text, or save/reopen the entire session as a file to pick up later.

Settings

Connect any AI provider — Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a custom endpoint — with a live connection test and a saved key per device.

Bring your own AI key

No new vendor, no markup, no data flowing through a middleman — your organization's own AI account powers every translation.

What it is not

Roark Health explains documentation a clinician already produced. It does not diagnose, interpret new symptoms, or recommend treatment — and it says so, clearly, inside the product itself.

Because your organization brings its own AI provider key, you're never paying us a markup on AI usage — you're paying for the platform itself. That's why we price in two straightforward ways, not by usage.

Small Clinic eligibility: one plan per practice, tied to a single tax ID/NPI and a single location. It is not available to multi-location systems or to multiple affiliated practices splitting providers across several Small Clinic plans — that use case should use Per-Provider Seat or Site License instead. We may ask for practice registration details to confirm eligibility.

Why no usage-based pricing: since your organization supplies its own AI provider API key, Roark never touches your AI spend or marks it up — you're billed by your own provider directly for usage, and by us only for the platform. We think that's the more transparent way to do it.
