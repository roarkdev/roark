The Product

A full patient-documentation workspace: paste in an after-visit summary, discharge note, or lab result, and work through it across six purpose-built tabs: Translate, Summary, Questions, Record, Export, and Settings.

Translate

Paste text directly, open a .txt file, or try a built-in example document — then generate a plain-language translation in one step.

Summary

Editable plain-language key points drawn only from the original document — add, edit, or remove any point, and copy the list with one click.

Questions

A personalized checklist of questions for the next appointment, based on what the document actually says — editable and copyable.

Record

A dedicated space for the patient's own notes during the appointment — kept alongside the document it relates to.

Export

Download as PDF, print directly, save as plain text, or save/reopen the entire session as a file to pick up later.

Settings

Connect any AI provider — Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a custom endpoint — with a live connection test and a saved key per device.

How it fits into a visit

Roark Health is run by your care team on a clinic device. Patients get the output — on paper or as a PDF — without needing an account, an app, or an AI key of their own.

  1. 1

    After the note is written

    A nurse, MA, or care coordinator pastes the after-visit summary, discharge note, or lab result into the Translate tab and generates a plain-language version.

  2. 2

    Staff review before anything is shared

    The Summary and Questions tabs are fully editable, so a team member checks the wording, removes anything that doesn't fit, and adds context the patient needs.

  3. 3

    The patient takes it home

    Print it or export a PDF for the patient. The question checklist goes with them to the next appointment, and the Record tab holds notes taken during that visit.

Security & Privacy

Where patient data goes

Clinical documents contain protected health information (PHI). Roark Health is built so that PHI goes directly from your browser to the AI provider your organization chooses — and nowhere else. Roark doesn't receive, process, or store any of it — no documents, no outputs, no usage data.

DataWhere it goesWhat Roark stores
Document text you paste or openSent from the browser directly to your organization's AI provider over HTTPSNothing
Plain-language output, summary, questions, and notesStays in the browser session; leaves the device only if you export or save itNothing
Saved session filesSaved to the device or location you choose, like any other fileNothing
Your AI provider API keyStored in that device's browser and sent only to the provider it belongs toNothing
Usage and activityNot tracked by Roark. Your AI provider's own console shows API usage for your keyNothing

HIPAA and your AI provider

Because PHI goes to the AI provider you connect, that provider is the one handling it on your behalf. Before using Roark Health with real patient documents, your organization needs a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) in place with that provider, and should use only the account, plan, or endpoint the BAA covers.

Use a BAA-covered account

Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google each offer BAAs for eligible business accounts. Confirm coverage with your provider before going live.

Routers and custom endpoints

OpenRouter and custom endpoints may route requests through additional parties. Use them with PHI only if every party in the path is covered by a BAA.

Test with sample documents

The built-in example documents contain no real patient data, so your team can evaluate the product during the free trial before any BAA is signed.

Questions IT and compliance teams ask

Does Roark sign a BAA?

Because Roark does not receive, store, or transmit PHI, we are not acting as a business associate for document content. If your compliance team would like that confirmed in writing, or requires a BAA as a matter of policy, contact us and we'll work through it with you.

How are API keys protected on shared workstations?

Keys are stored in the browser profile of the device where they were entered and are never sent to Roark. On shared workstations, we recommend a dedicated browser profile per role or user, standard OS-level logins, and a restricted API key from your provider with spending limits. Keys can be removed at any time from the Settings tab.

Can we restrict which AI providers staff can connect?

Site License customers can configure an organization-wide key and provider during onboarding so individual staff don't enter their own. Contact us to discuss your setup.

Does Roark use our documents to train AI models?

No. Roark never receives your documents. Whether your AI provider retains or trains on API data is governed by your agreement with that provider — most business API plans do not train on customer data by default, but confirm this in your contract.
